# Business in a Box

**A WhatsApp-first business management platform for Zimbabwean SMEs.**

Business in a Box is a multi-tenant SaaS platform designed to help
small and medium-sized businesses manage quotations, customers,
inventory, sales, payments, documents and jobs from one system.

## Project Status

🚧 Under active development — Phase 1: Project Foundation.

## Planned Features

- Company registration and multi-tenant accounts
- Customer and product management
- USD and ZiG pricing
- Inventory and stock movement tracking
- Quotations with version history
- Branded PDF, Word and Excel documents
- WhatsApp-based quotation sharing and acceptance records
- Sales, deposits and customer balances
- Supplier and purchase order management
- Job tracking and business reports
- FREE and PRO subscription plans
